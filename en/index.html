How it works

You practice with your voice, just like in a solfège class. The difference is that feedback comes right away.

1

See the score

Each exercise shows a short melody written for your level.

2

Read it or sing it

Say the name of each note with the metronome, or sing it.

3

Get your feedback

When you finish, you see which notes you got right, where you rushed or dragged, and how in tune you sang.

Everything a solfège exam asks for

A leveled curriculum that takes you from your first staff to conservatory entrance level.

Reading

Read notes out loud, in time, in treble and bass clef.

Rhythm

Tap rhythms on the screen with the metronome, including two-part rhythms.

Intonation

Sing melodies and the app measures your pitch note by note, in your voice type.

Ear training

Train your ear with intervals, chords, and melodic and rhythmic dictation.

Fixed do-re-mi, the way it is taught in Latin America

NoteSurfing uses fixed do, the system used in Mexico, Latin America and Spain. No letter names, no translating: you read the way a teacher would ask you to in class. The app is in Spanish.

Made by a musician

The curriculum was designed by a musicologist trained at Mexico's National Conservatory of Music, following conservatory solfège pedagogy.

Start today with Level 1

It's free. When you want to keep going, NoteSurfing PRO unlocks every level.
