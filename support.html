Frequently asked questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google, not by NoteSurfing.

  • iPhone: open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → NoteSurfing and tap Cancel Subscription.
  • Android: open Google Play → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → NoteSurfing and tap Cancel.

Cancellation takes effect at the end of the period you already paid for.

How do I restore a purchase?

In the App, open the NoteSurfing PRO screen (from any locked level, or Perfil → Ajustes → Desbloquear PRO) and tap Restaurar compras (Restore purchases). Make sure you use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.

The App doesn't detect my voice

  • Check that NoteSurfing has microphone permission in your phone's settings.
  • Close other apps that may be using the microphone, such as calls, Zoom or voice memos.
  • Practice somewhere quiet, without headphones.
  • If it still fails, fully close the App and open it again.

How do I change my voice type?

Go to Perfil → Ajustes → Tipo de voz (Profile → Settings → Voice type). Intonation exercises adjust to the voice you choose.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, tap ¿Olvidaste tu contraseña? (Forgot your password?) and enter your email. You'll get a link within a few minutes; check your spam folder too. If you signed up with Google or Apple, use that same button: there is no password to reset.

How do I delete my account?

In the App, go to Perfil → Ajustes → Eliminar cuenta (Profile → Settings → Delete account). Your account and progress are deleted and cannot be recovered. If you can't sign in, or if you also want us to delete your recordings, email privacy@notesurfing.com from your account's email address. Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription: cancel it in the App Store or Google Play too.

How do I report a bug or suggest something?

In the App, go to Perfil → Ajustes → Reportar un problema (Report a problem), or email support@notesurfing.com. It helps a lot if you include:

  • Your phone model and OS version.
  • The App version (shown in Ajustes).
  • What you were doing when it happened and, if possible, a screen recording.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their policies. Request them at reportaproblem.apple.com (iPhone) or from your Google Play purchase history (Android).
